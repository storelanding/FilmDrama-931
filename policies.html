1. Scope and service

This policy applies to the INCAI website and any INCAI service that links to it. “INCAI,” “we,” and “us” refer to the operator of the INCAI AI film showcase.

The website currently published is a static showcase. It does not offer account registration, in-site payments, watch-history profiles, chat, or survey forms. If these features are introduced, this policy will be updated before they are made available.

2. Information we handle

Information you choose to provide

If you email us, we receive the information in your message, such as your email address, name if included, and the details needed to respond. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Technical information

When a website is visited, the hosting provider may process standard connection and security data, such as IP address, date and time of the request, requested page, browser type, and device or network information. This data helps deliver the site, diagnose errors, and protect it from abuse. INCAI does not use this page to build user profiles or track film viewing activity.

Third-party page resources

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts and film artwork from images hosted at i.pinimg.com. When your browser requests those files, the relevant provider may receive technical connection data, including your IP address. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies and similar technologies

The current INCAI pages do not set first-party cookies or use local storage for analytics or advertising. Third-party services used to deliver page resources may apply their own technologies or keep request logs. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking external resources may affect how some page elements appear.

3. How we use information

We use information described in this policy only as reasonably needed to:

  • make the website and its requested resources available;
  • maintain site security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot technical issues;
  • read and respond to messages you send us; and
  • meet legal obligations or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not currently use this website to serve targeted advertising or to sell personal information.

4. Legal bases

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the website; on steps needed to respond to a request you make; on consent where it is required; and on legal obligations. You may contact us for more information about the basis relevant to a particular request.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We may share limited information with service providers that help us host, secure, or deliver the website and its resources, including the relevant hosting, font, and image-delivery providers. They may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect users or the public, to enforce our rights, or as part of a merger, restructuring, or transfer of the website or its operation. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention

We keep email correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain relevant business or legal records. Technical logs are retained according to the hosting provider’s operational and security practices. Retention periods may vary based on the nature of the information, security needs, and applicable legal requirements.

7. International transfers

INCAI’s hosting and resource providers may process technical information in countries other than the one where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, the relevant service provider is responsible for implementing them for its processing. Please review the provider policies linked below for further details.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to receive information you provided in a portable format; or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a request, email ltinc9986@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request before responding. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. These rights are subject to applicable legal limits and exceptions.

9. Security

We and our service providers use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information against accidental loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children

INCAI is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 through this website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate steps.

External websites and services are not operated by INCAI. This includes providers whose fonts or images load on our pages. We are not responsible for their privacy or security practices, and encourage you to review their policies:

We may update this policy as the website or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when the current version was published. If a material change affects how information is handled, we will take reasonable steps to provide notice on the website.

13. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints about how information is handled, contact INCAI at ltinc9986@gmail.com. We will review your message and respond as required by applicable law. You may also contact the data-protection authority in your place of residence.
